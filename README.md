# BLACKBOX — hacker MUD prototype

A virus breaks into your server; you fight it, trace where it came from, then make a run on that location: explore its file system with `ls`, `cd`, `cat` and `pull`, beat what guards it, and jack out to bank what you found. This build is **solo**; co-op comes later (see the design review in the Hacker MUD project).

**Rules live in [`GAME_RULES.md`](GAME_RULES.md).** That file is the single source of truth; this README only explains how to run and work on the code.

## Run

Needs Node.js. No packages, no build step.

```sh
node serve.mjs
```

Open http://127.0.0.1:4173. You land on the **Map**.

For isolated checks that never read or write the save, open `/?playtest=cryptjack`, `/?playtest=splinter`, `/?playtest=ghostroot` or `/?playtest=random` (fights start paused; type `resume`; add `&level=12` to fight at that level, tells and all), or `/?playtest=run` to start connected to a location (add `&template=mailhub|mirror|archive|lab`, `&family=worm|ghostroot` for the Nest / Hidden quirks, and `&depth=3` for deeper layers).

To skip straight to a run in your real save, type `developer location ransomware`, then click the new node on the Map and Connect. To try later skills and talents, `developer level 25` sets your class's level (your server's level is your highest class level; `developer server 10` sets a floor under it, for testing). To start over from level 1, type `reset game` (twice) or use Reset game on the System page; settings stay.

## Breach prototype

Phase 0 of the roguelite redesign ([`docs/roguelite.md`](docs/roguelite.md)): one breach of one server, playable end to end. Open `/?playtest=breach&class=breaker&level=10` (class `breaker`, `bastion`, `infiltrator` or `operator`; add `&sub=<subclass>` for another subclass and `&seed=<n>` to replay a map). It builds a fresh character at that level in blue gear, with two scripts, on a campaign that is never saved: your real save is not read, changed or migrated. Code: `dist/breach.mjs` (state, map, nodes, Trace, the hunter), `dist/scripts.mjs`, `dist/skillrules.mjs`, `dist/rewrites.mjs`, `dist/breach-view.mjs` and `dist/breach.css`.

- **The server.** MERIDIAN-MX-14, a TOLLGATE Mailhub at your level. Three acts (Perimeter, Services, Kernel) of five rows on a branching map, a gate after acts 1 and 2 (Watchdog or Crawler, then Sentinel or Shredder) and DEADBOLT, the Resident, at `/core`. Act 2 is a level over the server, act 3 two, DEADBOLT one.
- **The map.** Click a lit node. You read node kinds two rows ahead (an Infiltrator three); past that only the lanes show. Gates and DEADBOLT always show. Each fight row runs a subsystem (smtpd and sshd, cron and ledger, backup and kmod). The prompt still takes `ls`, `cd <n>`, `tree` and each screen's verbs (`pick 2`, `cat`, `pull`, `rest`, `reslot`, `buy 1`, `patch`, `service`, `open`, `force`, `splice`, `fight`, `enter`, `leave`, `choose 1`).
- **The screen.** A strip across the top holds the run: Signal, **Trace** (a meter with its alarm and hunt marks), tokens, keycards, the server kind's rule, the **Pack** (its count, and a dot while it holds gear you haven't looked at; click it for the pack and what's banked, each with Equip) and your **scripts** as chips (hover one for what it does). Beside the map is the one thing to decide (a rewrite, a cache, a vault, a switch, a broker, a terminal, a defrag, a gate, the end card), or a single line, *Pick your next node*, when the map is the choice. Under it the tty shows its last two lines; click it for the last twelve. A row's gutter names its subsystem and, once you rewrite it, the rewrite (hover for its Output). XP shows on the end card. On a phone the strip sticks to the top and the decision sits over the map.
- **Nodes.** Virus (a fight), elite (1.5× Integrity, two tells, loud), cache (tokens, and now and then a script; one in four is bait, so `cat` it first), defrag (rest for 35% Signal and a quieter Trace, or re-slot: the only place your bar changes), broker (GLASSJAW, Halcyon, Kestrel, NULL CHOIR or LANTERN: scripts and a Signal patch, for tokens; Kestrel scrubs your Trace and LANTERN sells the map of the act ahead), terminal (a half-written cron job, leaked creds, a honeytoken or a sandbox sample: each a choice with a cost).
- **Keycards and vaults.** Most acts hold a **vault** (V on the map) and, in an earlier row, a virus carrying a **keycard** (K): beat it and the card is yours. `open` a vault with a keycard (+15 Trace), or `force` it without one (10% of your max Signal and +35 Trace). A vault holds blue or yellow gear (a third of them) or an uncommon or better script, and a line of its author's logs.
- **Switches.** One an act (S): a **Patch server** (every virus left in the act loses 1 ◆ on every part) or a **Gate relay** (the act's gate, or in the last act the Resident, starts with 20% less Integrity and its first attack 2 cycles late). `fight` its watchdog ICE (a small fight; your Trace drops 20) or `splice` it with no fight (+15 Trace).
- **Trace and the hunter.** Trace (0 to 100) is how loud you've been. Elites, vaults, bait, honeytokens, a finished cron job and splices raise it; every quiet step lowers it by 3, a rest by 10. At 30 the alarm sounds (a Mailhub floods spam from here), and at 55 you're **traced**: a hunter ICE (H, a level over the node it lands on) drops a few rows ahead and moves one node toward you for every move you make. If it reaches you, you fight it; beat it and it drops a script and your Trace falls to 15. Get your Trace back under 30 and it loses you. Infiltrators raise Trace at half the rate.
- **Signal** is the breach's health. It carries from fight to fight, and only a defrag, a patch or a script restores it; healing inside a fight never takes you above the Signal you brought into it. At 0 the breach ends: the unbanked pack is lost, XP, banked gear and your scripts stay. A gate banks the pack, then you go on or jack out.
- **Scripts.** One-shot exploits you carry between breaches, 3 slots (Archive adds more): Sasser (restores 20% Signal), KRACK (a 20% shield), Shellshock (the soonest attack 3 cycles late), Spectre (Exposes every part for 2 cycles), BlueKeep (breaks 1 ◆ on every part), Slowloris (every attack 2 cycles late), Conficker (your whole bar ready), Mirai (3 helpers at a part), Ripple20 (calls off every tell winding up), Meltdown (30% of a part's max Integrity through armor). Run one in a fight from its button or with `script <n> [part]` (or `run <name>`): it takes no cycle, and one a fight. They drop from vaults, elites (a quarter of the time), hunters and Residents (half), sometimes sit in caches, and brokers sell them (35, 55 or 85 tokens by rarity). A full set leaves a new one behind.
- **Rewrites.** Win a subsystem's fight and pick 1 of 2 rewrites. A rewrite does nothing on this breach: its *Output* is what the captured server will do for you (shown on the capture card). An elite's is tier II, and clearing a subsystem twice raises it to II.
- **Server kinds.** Every server kind has one rule, shown on its card, on the strip and on the map: **Mailhub** (Spam floods: once your Trace reaches 30, every fight brings a weak, quick Spam Flood part), **Archive** (Deep storage: two vaults and two keycards an act), **Relay** (Hops: two open shortcuts an act that skip a row, drawn as curves), **Mirror** (Mirrors: one cache an act is a mirror, with an elite behind it) and **Lab** (Overheat: from the 10th cycle of a fight the rack burns you for 2% of your max Signal a cycle and every part for 3% of its max Integrity).
- **Tells.** A breach virus brings one tell kind, an elite two (a charge first); gates and DEADBOLT keep their own. **Overclock** is a cast (SIGINT or two hits in its window). If it lands, every part takes double damage for 2 cycles and every attack comes a cycle sooner, so a big cooldown can cash in. **Lock** has no answer but breaking its part. When it lands, the first command you fire after it is locked for 3 cycles, key 1 included, so feed it a skill that is cooling anyway. Breach fights enrage too: a virus from cycle 8, an elite 9, a gate 10 (Residents keep their own).
- **The end.** DEADBOLT drops its loot (a white or better protocol, its uniques on their own roll, and a script half the time), then the capture card lists each subsystem's rewrite and output (stock where you never cleared it), the heat, the XP, the loot banked, your peak Trace, the vaults you opened and `core.dump`, a TOLLGATE fragment.
- **The sim.** `node breachsim.mjs [runs] [level]` plays whole breaches with the planner and the breach bot and prints win rates per class (`breach.test.mjs` keeps every class over 25% and the mean between 45% and 85%); `node breachsim.mjs subs` and `servers` print them by subclass and by server.

## Breach campaign

Phase 2 of the roguelite redesign ([`docs/roguelite.md`](docs/roguelite.md)): a campaign of breaches you play from level 1. Open `/?campaign=breach`, or **Breach campaign** beside *Try a breach* on the map (it opens in a new tab). It has its own save (`blackbox-campaign-v1` in localStorage): the old game's save is never read, written or migrated, and the old game's systems (map, mail, server, hubs, invasions, firewall, memory, markets, anything on a real-time clock) do nothing in it. Code: `dist/campaign.mjs` (the servers, outputs, checkpoints, bounties, the Archive), `dist/campaign-view.mjs` and `dist/campaign.css`, on top of the breach prototype's files.

- **First launch.** You log in with a handle, read a short transmission from Halcyon and pick a class. You start at level 1 with a white protocol in every slot and one script (Sasser). The tabs are **Campaign**, **Loadout**, **Archive** and **System**, plus **Breach** and **Fight** while one runs. Tips are off, and the prompt only takes fight, breach, gear and skill commands (`help` lists them).
- **The map.** 14 servers in 4 layers, each server at a fixed level: The Sprawl (1 to 5), Backhaul (6 to 11), The Stacks (12 to 17) and Deep Core (18 to 25). Lines link neighbours. You can breach SPRAWL-00 and any server linked to one you hold. A server linked to one you can breach shows as *unknown*, and the rest are hidden until you get close. The strip on top counts what you hold, your breaches, your scripts (and slots) and the Archive, and lists every output your network runs (hover a chip for what it does).
- **A server's card.** Click a server. Its layer, level (coloured against yours), kind and author; its kind's **Rule**; its **Resident** (hover for its phases) and **Loot** (its Collection: its own uniques, or for a native boss your network's natives open at its level, and the chance the next kill drops one); the subsystems it **Runs** by act; your **Best** result there; the outputs that reach it (**With you**); its outputs once you hold it; its **bounties**; and **Breach** (**Re-image** once you hold it, **Breach from gate N** while a checkpoint holds).
- **Breaches by level.** SPRAWL-00 is the tutorial: one act of three rows, then RELAY-KING. A server under level 6 runs one act of five rows and its Resident, 6 to 9 two acts with a gate between, 10 and up the full three. Viruses are at the server's level (act 2 one over, act 3 two over) and the Resident one over. The Residents, in order: RELAY-KING, BACK ORIFICE, VAULT WARDEN, PATCH TUESDAY, REPO MAN, HOLLOW CHOIR, DEADBOLT, MIRRORSHADE, TRIPMINE, FLOODWALL, HASHLORD, SLEEPWALKER, ECHOLALIA and the UNDERWRITER. Every fight past the tutorial has more Integrity and hits harder by layer (×1.1, ×1.2, ×1.28, ×1.36). Lock tells wait for level 5.
- **XP.** A kill pays the breach's XP, the Resident three times over and a gate half again. A capture adds a third of the breach's kill XP. About ten breaches take a class from level 1 to 10.
- **Loot.** Gear comes from elites, gates, vaults and the Resident, never from a plain virus, a draft or a bounty: about one to two pieces banked a breach, a real upgrade (a better rarity, or three levels up) every two or three breaches. Blue and yellow gear from a breach can roll a rare skill rule for your class (see *Breach map, scripts and heat*).
- **Outputs.** A capture keeps each subsystem's rewrite, and its output runs on every later breach. Each rewrite counts once, at the best tier you hold. Spam Cannon, Sinkhole and Testbed only reach servers linked to their own, and Jump Host lets you breach servers two links past its server (three at tier II) before they're revealed. Mail Drop mails you a script each capture (uncommon or better at tier II), Forged Keys starts every breach with a keycard (two at II), Nightly Build lowers your Trace at every gate, Archive adds script slots and Kernel Hook thins the Resident. The rest do what their Output line says: Slush Fund starts you with tokens, Range puts *Replay* on the card, and so on.
- **Losing and coming back.** A loss keeps your XP, banked gear and scripts, loses the unbanked pack, and leaves the server uncaptured. A gate you beat is a **checkpoint**: the card then offers *Breach from gate N* (a fresh map from the next act, with the rewrites you picked in the acts behind it and full Signal) or *From the start*. A capture clears it. Jacking out at a gate keeps it too.
- **Re-imaging.** Breach a server you hold again. Each subsystem you clear shows the rewrite it runs now (*Runs now*): **Keep** it, at the better of the two tiers, or rewrite it. Subsystems you don't clear keep theirs (*kept* on the capture card). The Resident is the same fight, and its `core.dump` is already yours.
- **Bounties.** Up to 2 on each card (none on SPRAWL-00, 3 with Bounty Board), posted by GLASSJAW, Halcyon, Kestrel and LANTERN: capture without letting a tell land, without resting at a defrag, without a hunter finding you, or without ending a fight under half Signal; clear an elite, or open a vault, on the way. Click **Take** on one before you breach. Met, it pays a script, uncommon or better, and a new bounty goes up. Missed, it costs nothing.
- **The Archive.** Each Resident's first fall leaves a `core.dump` in `/core`, shown on the capture card and filed in the Archive in story order: 14 fragments in 7 threads (LOWLIGHT, SWARMLINE, Halcyon, TOLLGATE, NULL CHOIR, GLASSJAW, PALEMASK). Missing ones show only as a count.
- **The breach page.** As in the prototype. While the map is the choice, the column beside it lists the lit nodes as cards: kind, subsystem, author, level, what you know of it and what it leads to. Click a card or the node. The end card adds the bounty, the mailed script, the servers the capture opened and a kept checkpoint, and **Back to the map**.
- **The sim.** `node campaignsim.mjs [breaches] [seeds]` plays the campaign from level 1 with each class (a sensible bot: the lowest server it can breach, re-imaging the deepest one it holds when the next is more than a level over it, retrying from checkpoints, Payload, Warden, Demolitionist and Herder at 10) and prints breaches to levels 5, 10, 15 and 20, minutes at about 5 seconds a cycle and 20 a node, win rates by level band, loot per breach (banked, swaps and real upgrades) and where runs die. `campaign.test.mjs` keeps every class reaching level 10 within 22 breaches.

## Breach map, scripts and heat

Phase 3 of the roguelite redesign ([`docs/roguelite.md`](docs/roguelite.md) 9.3 and 11): breadth, without drafting. Power inside a breach no longer comes from drafted mods and CVEs; it comes from the gear you bank, the scripts you carry and the route you take through the map. Rolled genomes run on every breach node, and heat on top. It runs in the prototype (`?playtest=breach`, add `&heat=<0-8>`) and the campaign alike. Code: `dist/breach.mjs`, `dist/scripts.mjs`, `dist/skillrules.mjs`, `dist/heat.mjs`, `dist/genes.mjs` (`rollGenome`), `dist/rewrites.mjs`, `dist/campaign.mjs`; the bots in `breachsim.mjs` and `campaignsim.mjs`.

- **The route is the choice.** A map shows its vaults and switches, the viruses that carry keycards and, once you're traced, the hunter. Taking a vault costs Trace (or Signal), a switch is a fight or a loud splice, and every loud move brings the hunter nearer. A breach where you stay quiet is a breach where the hunter never comes.
- **Skill rules on gear.** Eight of the old mods are now rare rules a blue or yellow item can roll, for your class only (the old game never rolls them): Aftershock (Crack deals 5–12 damage for each ◆ it breaks), Zero Click (Exploit deals 4–10 to every other part and Exposes them all), Backpressure (Rate Limit delays the target's next attack a cycle), Reflective ACL (a hit your shield absorbs whole bounces back at 100–160%), Long Poll (Keepalive ticks every burn on the target once more), Viral Load (Inject's burn grows 2–6 a tick), Snare (Hook delays the target's next attack a cycle) and Daemonize (Deploy's helper deals 8–14 a cycle for the rest of the fight). Reforging never gives one.
- **Phantom talents.** Two old Infiltrator mods filled the Phantom's thin talent choices: Ghost Route (Null Route also delays the attack that lands soonest by 2 cycles, at tier 2) and Tracking Pixel (a capstone: when a Tagged part breaks, Tag moves to the part whose attack lands soonest). They replace Rotating Proxies and Leaked Creds, which only worked on a run.
- **Rolled genomes.** Every wild and elite virus on a breach rolls its genes from its author's toolkit (signature genes three to one), within the level's budget less 2 points and the compatibility rules (genes.mjs `rollGenome`): its third part and at most one mutation, two on an elite, none under level 4. A mutation lives on a part (a hot tag on it names it): break that part and the rule ends. Gates are fixed guards and Residents keep their hand-written genes. A node's card shows its genes as chips, ??? until you've met them (Audit Trail names them all).
- **Heat 1 to 8.** Each rank keeps the ones under it and adds one modifier: 1 Loud (fights have 15% more Integrity and hit 10% harder), 2 Hardened ICE (gates bring one more tell, Lock), 3 Rotation (one more elite an act), 4 Thin pipe (rest restores 20%), 5 Audit (breaches start with your Trace at 30), 6 Fog of war (you read 1 row ahead, 2 as an Infiltrator), 7 Tripwire (your Trace rises 50% faster), 8 Hardened Resident (25% more Integrity, and it re-arms every part at 80%). Each rank pays: gear drops a level higher (up to 5), yellows drop more often, and kills pay 25% more XP. The strip shows **Heat** (hover for every modifier); the capture card names it.
- **Heat in the campaign.** A server card has a heat picker (−, the rank, +) once heat 1 is open, with the modifiers in force as chips. Capturing any server at your highest heat opens the next rank. Each server records the best heat you captured it at (`H2` on its node), and re-imaging asks for that heat or more. The strip shows the highest rank open.
- **No sustain between fights.** Only a defrag, a broker's patch and Sasser raise your breach Signal. Heals inside a fight stop at what you came in with, and your Signal bar shows it: past that point the bar is hatched behind a bright edge (hover it for the cap).
- **Rewrites (20 over 9 subsystems).** Output only. sshd Service Account (jack out at any defrag with your pack), dns Zone Transfer (read 1 row further) and Sinkhole (linked servers' viruses roll only genes you've decoded), ledger Bounty Board (one more bounty on every card), syslog Audit Trail (node cards name every gene) and Listening Post (Residents drop their uniques 50% more often), sandbox Testbed (linked servers roll a gene point more and drop gear 2 levels higher) and Range (*Replay* on its card: the Resident alone, for its loot). Relays run dns, Mirrors syslog and Labs sandbox.
- **The campaign save** stays `blackbox-campaign-v1`. A save from before this migrates in place on load: the draft pool, mods and rerolls are dropped, the CVEs it held become scripts (up to 3; Sasser if none carry over), and a breach in flight gets its Trace, keycards, switches and stats, and loses any draft on screen.
- **The bots.** `breachsim.mjs` plays breaches with the planner, the route bot (keycards, vaults, switches, hunter avoidance) and the script bot; `campaignsim.mjs` plays the campaign and prints loot per breach by level. `mapmech.test.mjs` holds the map, Trace, scripts, server kinds and save migration.

## Screens

**One name per thing.** On screen an enemy is a **virus** (never a process, stray or hostile), what it's made of are its **parts** (never modules), and armor is **◆** (never chits or encryption; Encrypt is the Encryptor's attack). Outpost modules are a different thing and keep their name. GAME_RULES.md uses "chit" for one ◆ as an internal word.

**Show, don't tell.** The screen shows state with numbers, bars, chips, pips and icons; explanations live in hover tooltips (`title`), never in sentences on the page. A count is `×N`, a comparison is a coloured `▲/▼ %` chip, progress is a bar, a cost is have/need chips, a state is a tag. Empty states are a word or two. New UI follows this; text that explains goes into a tooltip.

The top bar shows **Map, Mail, Server, Craft, Loadout, System** always (Daemons is a Loadout tab), **Consortium** when you're in one, plus **Fight** and **Run** only while a fight or run is live. Halcyon's store is its hub's **Shop** window (`connect halcyon`).

- **Crew column:** a narrow left column (`#sidebar`, `crewWindowMarkup`) on every page while you have a crew; none when solo. **Map cards** pop up beside the clicked node (`placeMapPop` in app.js). The top bar meters are Integrity, Signal and Credits, split by short rules; the class level is on the Loadout page. Your server on the map is the server glyph (two racks).
- **Map:** your server at the centre (its card shows the server's level, which is your highest class level, with that class's name, and on hover what the next level adds); traced locations radiate out by family (ransomware, worm, ghostroot), deeper layers branch off the node that led to them, and half-traced leads show as dashed ghosts with their %. A waiting intrusion pulses at the server's gate. An invasion moves in from its location to your wall as a spiked virus (a thief toward the outpost it's after), its kind under its name: amber outline on its way, red at your wall, throbbing with rings on a breach (and a ring fires out of the pager's lamp). Its card says what it does, what your wall will do and what it pays. Click any node for its card (server stats and repair, Engage, Connect, Jack in). Each server's level is the big number under it, coloured by how hard it is for you (green below, yellow even, orange and red above), with its layer as a small teal tag. Scroll to zoom (up to 5×, around the cursor), drag to pan, double-click or ⤢ to zoom back out. **Aa** (or `map names hover|on`) shows names only on hover.
- **The pager** (top bar): the latest world event scrolling on its screen, a lamp and an unread count; click it for Comms, the filtered list with links to where each thing happened.
- **Mail:** your standing with Halcyon Mutual, Indemnity and the retainer countdown; your contracts (held/3), the board (offers with their time left) and letters (unread count on the tab). Open any of them to read it; a contract shows its progress, pay, and Take / Deliver / Drop. On the map, takeover and recovery targets show as "contract", servers you've taken over as "yours", unknown servers you've heard of as "?" (flagged ones in yellow).
- **Store:** Halcyon's own line (relays, the Indemnified chase protocols, locked by tier), your kit (relays, key crackers, trace injectors) and four slots of agency stock with supplier, price trend, quantity and time left.
- **Server:** your server's level (your highest class level, with the class as a tag) and what the next level adds, code (Cipher, Worm, Kernel, Exploits), the **Firewall** (its level, its +N tier over the network's base and Upgrade, the fragmentation grid and Defrag, harden.sh, *Vulnerable to lv N+*, the ruler of what it blocks and holds, your signatures and streak, the current invasion with its kind, warning and bounty, Jack in or Intercept, Degraded mode, and its filters: two slots and one more at each of tiers +1, +3 and +5), the install queue with its progress bar, running services (upgrade or uninstall) and the services you can build. Installs run in real time, even while the game is closed. Testing: `developer code 50` gives code, `developer finish` completes the current install, `developer invade [kind]` puts an invasion at your wall (a thief sets out), `developer wall <n>|off` pins your wall's base, `developer crash` crashes the server, `developer event [courier|bounty|outbreak|leak]` deals an event now.
- **Loadout:** four class tabs, each with its own level; your class, passive, equipped skills, protocols and talents are all live. **Protocols** tab (opens first): left, the stash as one row per item (rarity edge, name, slot and level, stats; filter by slot; Load / Swap and deconstruct), loaded items not repeated; right, the class in use's protocol slots (4, 5 at 15, 6 at 30) and the stat sheet (every stat, zeros dimmed). **Skills and talents** tab: left, your level and XP under the class name (with a *Behind* tag while kills pay +50% on a level that runs long), the key bar you'll fight with (key 1 under your class's name, Bash, Ban, Poke or Ping, + 7 equipped skills; locked keys show the level they open at), your subclass's presets as chips over it (click one to put it on, *Save bar as…* to keep the bar under a name; `loadout use|save|list|show|delete`, docs/kits.md) and the class's library of skills (equip, unequip; locked ones show their level); right, the talent tree (teal = picked, amber = you can pick, gray = locked), with the class's sync bonus (what a command fired in a Sync Window adds for it, yellow) and its kit talent from level 5 under its passive. The class picker shows each class's sync bonus too, and when one lands it floats up with SYNCED (*SYNCED · +1 ◆*). Compiling protocols is on the Craft page.
- **System:** speed / sound / motion / vibration / shell (immersive or plain) / casing / tips (on, off, replay), a sound test, Reset game, the wire ticker, recent fight reports and the full log.
- **No how-to text on screens.** Names, numbers and state only; rules on hover; first-time tips (`dist/tips.mjs`) teach each thing once when it first appears. Playtest URLs skip them; `tips off` in the command line stops them.

### The combat screen

- **HUD (top):** the virus's name, family, what it threatens and its mutation, then two bars side by side: the Virus (sum of its parts) and yours (your server at home, your Signal on a run), with the virus's armor chits left under its bar.
- **Board (main):** one row per part — health, armor chits (◆ filled, ◇ broken), tags (veiled, exposed, weak); a part whose armor is broken shows faint cracks and its attacks placed in the cycle columns Now / +1 / +2 / +3. A tell (a move a part announces ahead, tells.mjs) sits on its part's row in the column it lands, styled like a crew boss's chip, with what answers it on its last line (*▸ Burst 47 or ◆2 in 2*), even on a veiled part. It can only be answered in its window, the cycle it lands (its last two at levels 1 to 5, and for an elite's or a boss's charge and a cast). While the window is open the chip turns the Sync Window's yellow with a NOW badge, and a longer window marks the cells before it with a WINDOW strip. Before a cycle resolves, every bar shows what it's about to lose as a blinking white slice: each part (from your command and the crew's; a hit on armor only breaks a chit, and the chits about to break blink too), the virus total, your Signal or server and each crewmate's (from the attacks you can see landing this cycle, after chits, shields and Block; crits aren't guessed). The *You* row shows your queued command and the countdown; under it, the *Status* row shows what's running on you (Momentum, Brace and other buffs, Scrambled) as a bar over the cycles it lasts. Red = lands this cycle, gold = next cycle; a dashed ◆ patch chip marks when a bare part gets a chit back. Click a row to target that part.
- **Side:** the virus art. **Log** under the board.
- **Net (runs):** one terminal column. Signal is in the header and in the prompt itself (`[47/50] location:/path$`), turning gold then red as it drops. Arriving in a directory lists it automatically; every name is clickable (directory = cd, file = cat, with a pull button on takeable files). The buttons under the prompt show only the actions that make sense where you are. `tree` prints a map, `pack` your unbanked loot, `help` the commands. The terminal shows one folder at a time (from the `cd` that got you there); `history` (or the *earlier* link at the top) shows the whole run. A typo'd name or command gets a clickable "Did you mean …?", in the terminal and in fights. Guard fights switch to the combat screen and back.
- **Loadout → Daemons tab** (between Protocols and Skills and talents): your daemon slots and the daemons you've found.
- **Gain cards and new counts:** a pull pops a small card with what you got; jacking out shows a Banked card with everything you brought home. Loadout, Craft and Daemons carry a teal count of what's new until you open them. A win at least a quarter faster than your usual pace (per class) pays +25% XP, a gold *Fast kill* row on the spoils card.
- **Command line + tray:** type, or press 1–9, 0 or - on an empty line to prefill an ability with the selected part, then Enter. Tab completes, ↑↓ recall history. Key 1 is your class's plain hit (Bash, Ban, Poke or Ping; `spike` works for everyone), 2–8 your seven equipped class skills, 9 the eighth (from level 22), 0 the ninth (from level 30), and - SIGINT (everyone's interrupt, from level 10, for casts). Keys appear as you unlock them, and a ghost key shows what the next level brings. A key whose moment is on the board says so in amber (situations.mjs), and a key a landed tell knocked offline says OFFLINE. Plan up to 3 cycles with `;` (`exploit enc; overload enc`). An empty cycle is filled by your daemons, then by key 1 on the last part you hit. Enter on an empty line resolves the cycle now; the top-bar button switches speed (12s / 8s / 5s per cycle).

Red is used only for "lands this cycle" and critical server health.

## Checks

```sh
node --test            # every *.test.mjs, including balance.test.mjs (class balance by level)
node playtest.mjs      # kill orders vs enemies → docs/PLAYTEST.md
node balance.mjs       # one scripted player per class at five points on the level curve → docs/BALANCE.md
node farmsim.mjs 18 30 # crews in the farm, with and without a Sysop and a Warden
# balance.test.mjs and loop.test.mjs guard the class band and the fight mix; targets not met yet are `todo` tests
```

The playtest script runs 7 scripted strategies against each fixture and 30 random variants. It checks that no single kill order always wins; it can't tell you if the game is fun. Play it with people for that.
