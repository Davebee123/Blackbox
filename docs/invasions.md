# Invasions: what's left

Invasions and daemons are built: the rules are in GAME_RULES.md "Home". The wall, its tiers and filters went in November
2026 (the designer: HOME is its health, invasions attack it, you intercept them); what's below about them is history.
This sheet keeps the numbers to watch and what's still worth doing.

## To watch

- **Daemon odds** (`DAEMON_DROP`): 35% when you stop an invasion, 50% when you intercept one, 15% at the wall, a sure
  one from a capture, +5% a stop without one. Lower the wall's 15% if a strong wall rains daemons; the pity keeps it fair.
- **The Lifeline** (band 5): a cheat-death, even small, can start to feel mandatory.
- **The chip at 1** (November 2026): Integrity is one bar with your runs, and an invasion's chip stops
  at 1; a lost home fight leaves you at 1. Crashes, Degraded mode, reboot sickness and the occupied home are gone. Watch
  whether a breach left alone still asks for you: at 20% of max a minute the rest outruns the chip.
- **Home fights on the one bar:** max Integrity is max Signal's (power and gear), well under the old server curve at
  high levels, while home viruses keep their home scaling. Watch how hard jacking in feels.
- **No invasions while logged off** (November 2026): nothing sets out, travels or chips on time away; every minute away
  banks Rested.

## Still worth doing

- A pager filter for just the wall, now that blocked invasions add quiet lines.
- A sound of its own for a scout finishing, in place of the general loss cue.
- An `invasions` panel on the Map listing the last few and what each paid, so the streak has a history.
