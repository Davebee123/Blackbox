# Receipts

**Status.** Designed and prototyped (look picked), not built. Lore: docs/lore.md 8.4 (*Vaults*).

## The rule

- **Every traced server's vault holds one receipt:** the frozen trade its Resident orphaned. One line:
  `TRD-77-0418877 · Corvid Exchange → Harrow Street Credit Union · ¤41,200 · 3 Apr 2077 · C+16`. Institutions from
  lore.md 8.1; the date fits the floor's era (lore.md 1).
- **The takeover card** shows the receipt and five faction stamps. Pick one:

| Stamp | Faction | In the world |
|---|---|---|
| **File** | Halcyon | It becomes a claim; Halcyon pays and keeps the account. |
| **Seal** | Kestrel | Walled off with the floor it froze on. |
| **Sell** | GLASSJAW | Sold on to the highest bidder. |
| **Settle** | LANTERN | Closed the way it was meant to close. |
| **Roll back** | NULL CHOIR | Both sides get their money back, decades late. |

- **Every stamp pays the same amount:** +10 rep (docs/factions-plan.md: receipts and contracts are rep's two sources) with its faction (the usual ripples) and one receipt toward that
  faction's piece (Faction gear). **3 receipts make one part**, beside the parts feuds give. No credits on any stamp.
  Halcyon's pull is the retainer its standing already pays; the piece you want is the reason to stamp elsewhere.
- **Later** keeps the receipt in your files. Hand it in at Halcyon's hub; each other hub takes it once found. No
  deadline.
- **Rivals:** a rival who gets away leaves the receipt.

## The look (picked: *Stamps*)

The takeover card: the amber kicker (*STACKS-31 · TAKEN OVER*), the receipt in an inset mono block (id, payer → payee,
sum in amber, date, *awaiting counterparty* dim), then five square stamps in a row, each its faction's mark, verb and
three pips toward its next part, in the faction's colour. **Later** sits under them. A stamp's tooltip: the verb, *<faction>
· Receipt*, one sentence on what happens to the trade, the chips (mark +4, pips and the piece's name), and
*▸ Three receipts make one part.* Rejected: *Rows* (one row a faction) and *Banner* (marks on the takeover banner).

## Payoff, later

The Ledger's Receipts page (each trade and how you closed it), the badge's tally (*31 settled · 12 filed*), and
wick's letter quoting it (docs/story-script.md). Each waits until the designer has played receipts.
