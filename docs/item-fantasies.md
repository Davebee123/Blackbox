# Item fantasies

Every set and non-set unique serves one power fantasy or purpose, with Diablo 2 and WoW as touchstones; a new one joins
a fantasy here. Combos fit the six slots, at their highest piece's level. Worth: docs/unique-roles.md, docs/set-roles.md;
stacking: docs/item-builds.md.

## Class fantasies

### Wrecking Ball (Demolitionist, 4–50)

Every break sets off the next. D2: Corpse Explosion. WoW: Arms: Sweeping Strikes.

- **Sets:** Wrecking Crew, Catchment
- **Uniques:** Eviction Notice 4, Jackhammer 5, Hashboard 7, Log4Shell 24, Black Ledger 26, Kill Chain 30, Culvert 40, Floodgate 41, Check Valve 44, Rockfall 45, Firmware 46
- **Back in a cycle** (7): Eviction Notice + Hashboard. Each break readies the skill that made it and takes a cycle off the rest.
- **Chain reaction** (46): Floodgate + Rockfall + Firmware + Culvert + Storm Drain + Basin [Catchment (2)]. Each break: +10% crit, +8% Clock Speed, the next attack a cycle late, leftover damage carried on.
- **Demolition crew** (50): Crowbar + Det Cord + Hard Hat + Blast Map + Firmware + Check Valve [Wrecking Crew (4)]. After a break, your next skill also hits the part beside it for 50%.

## Loose fits

- **Regular:** Echo has no fantasy of its own. Make its echoes break 2 ◆ on armor (Sapper).
- **Echo Chamber:** Only half a Reader piece. An answered tell makes your next hit echo.
- **Mixed Bag:** A stat bonus with a condition. Tie it to one kind of special.
- **Field Guide:** A stat bonus with a condition. Let it read a strain's tell.
- **Blank Check:** Hits the healthiest part, unlike the other Executioner pieces. Move it to the Opening Act.
- **Cryptominer:** Ramps like Time and a Half. Keep both: one ramps speed, the other damage.

## Gaps

- **Executioner:** All exploits but two Atoll pieces, so they never combine.
- **Sapper:** 9 of 12 are exploits; nothing past 42.
- **Conductor:** Nothing past 43; Hollow Note and Tail Circuit share the exploit slot.
- **Vampire:** No set; nothing past 44.
- **Redline:** No set of its own.
- **Crewmate:** One set and one unique, both by 43.
- **Cleave:** Four uniques, the first at 26.
- **Reader:** Nothing past 42.
