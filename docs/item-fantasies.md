# Item fantasies

Every set and non-set unique serves one power fantasy (a way of fighting it makes you great at) or one purpose (a job
you swap it in for), with Diablo 2 and WoW as the touchstones. A new unique or set joins a fantasy here. Each combo fits
one exploit, proxy, shell and script and two implants, and its level is its highest piece's. What each piece is worth:
docs/unique-roles.md, docs/set-roles.md; how items stack: docs/item-builds.md.

## Class fantasies

### Wrecking Ball (Demolitionist, 4–50)

Every part you break sets off the next. D2: The Necromancer's Corpse Explosion: one kill sets off the pack. WoW: Arms warrior: Sweeping Strikes, then Execute down the line.

- **Sets:** Wrecking Crew, Catchment
- **Uniques:** Eviction Notice 4, Jackhammer 5, Hashboard 7, Log4Shell 24, Black Ledger 26, Kill Chain 30, Culvert 40, Floodgate 41, Check Valve 44, Rockfall 45, Firmware 46
- **Back in a cycle** (7): Eviction Notice + Hashboard. A break readies the skill that made it and takes a cycle off the rest, so a four-part virus hands you four refunds.
- **Chain reaction** (46): Floodgate + Rockfall + Firmware + Culvert + Storm Drain + Basin [Catchment (2)]. Each break gives +10% crit and +8% Clock Speed for 3 cycles (each stacks twice), pushes the next attack back a cycle and carries up to 60 leftover damage onto the next part. Storm Drain and Basin add Evasion and Restore for every part down.
- **Demolition crew** (50): Crowbar + Det Cord + Hard Hat + Blast Map + Firmware + Check Valve [Wrecking Crew (4)]. After a break your next damaging skill also hits the part beside it for 50%, and Det Cord gives you a ◆ for 2 cycles. Firmware speeds you up on every break, and Check Valve takes 40% off the parts that blow up when they go.

### Redline (Overclocker, 8–50)

Fill Momentum, crit, and fire your biggest skill again. D2: The Frenzy Barbarian, faster with every hit. WoW: Fire mage's Hot Streak: crits hand you the next big spell.

- **Uniques:** Shrapnel 8, Hot Reload 30, Ballast 42, Riprap 42, Pumphouse 44, Fuse Map 46, Microcode 48, Undervolt 49, Root of Trust 50
- **Hot streak** (50): Hot Reload + Ballast + Root of Trust + Pumphouse. At full Momentum Ballast adds 10% crit. Each crit readies the skill again (Hot Reload) and stacks +4% damage, five times (Root of Trust). Spend the Momentum on your biggest skill for +8% a stack (Pumphouse).
- **Meltdown** (49): Fuse Map + Undervolt + Ballast + Microcode. Every break adds an extra Momentum, so it fills fast, and at full your crits come 10% more often and hit 15% harder. Undervolt stops Momentum raising the damage you take, so you can sit at full all fight, and Microcode adds 6% Clock Speed and a cycle off your shortest cooldown.

### Bulwark (Warden, 6–50)

Every hit comes to you, and your Block grows with each one. D2: The Paladin behind Holy Shield and a Stormshield. WoW: Protection warrior: Shield Block up, Revenge on every hit taken.

- **Fortress** (48): Siltstone + Interrupt Vector + Seawall + Cold Iron. Answer a tell and you take a ◆ for 2 cycles, and each ◆ you hold is 2 Block. Every hit your Block reduces adds 1 more for the fight, up to 5, and Cold Iron lets Block take its full amount off burns and encryption.
- **Vengeance** (49): Rider + Copay + Exclusion + Premium + Moral Hazard + Ground Plane [Fine Print (4)]. Let the tells you can't answer land. Each one shields you for 5% and adds 1 Block, 2% crit and 3% damage, and behind the shield you hit 10% harder and crit 8% more often. Every hit on you adds 2% crit on top.
- **Strongbox** (50): Drill + Combination + Vault Door + Tumbler + Siltstone + Breakwater [Strongbox (4)]. Your shields are 25% larger, the first hit each fight deals half, and a hit for over 15% marks its part to take 10% more from you while you heal 3%. Breakwater turns each shield into damage, and Siltstone turns ◆ into Block.

## Loose fits

- **Regular:** Echo has no fantasy of its own, so this sits in Sapper because an echo on armor breaks another ◆. Make its echo break 2 ◆ on armor, so it is a Sapper piece outright.
- **Echo Chamber:** More Echo while a tell winds up only half fits the Reader. Have an answered tell make your next hit echo for certain.
- **Mixed Bag:** Damage for each special on the server: a stat stick with a condition. Tie it to one kind of special so it hunts something.
- **Field Guide:** Damage and crit against any strain: the same. Give it an effect that reads a strain's tell, so it becomes the strain hunter's piece.
- **Blank Check:** Hits the healthiest part, the opposite of the other Executioner pieces. Fine as an opener; or move it to the Opening Act with a first-hit clause.
- **Cryptominer:** Ramps like Time and a Half, on Clock Speed instead of damage. Keep both: one ramps speed and the other damage, and they combine.

## Gaps

- **Executioner:** Every Executioner piece but Atoll's Lagoon and Reef is an exploit, so they never combine.
- **Sapper:** 9 of the 12 Sapper uniques are exploits, and the three scripts (Regular, Lien, Lockpick) are level 6 to 8. Nothing past level 42.
- **Conductor:** Nothing past level 43, and Hollow Note and Tail Circuit (odd and even cycles) share the exploit slot.
- **Vampire:** No set, and nothing past level 44.
- **Redline:** No set of its own past the Wrecking Crew tier set; Burst Buffer and Bootloader are the nearest.
- **Crewmate:** One set and one unique, both by level 43.
- **Cleave:** Four uniques, the first at level 26.
- **Reader:** Sixteen uniques, none past level 42.
